## What it does

`main.ts` writes one two-line explanation to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A queue is a slow train made of people, where each person is a carriage holding the spot behind the one in front.
>
> It maps the order and the waiting — everyone moves only when the person ahead moves — but it leaves out that carriages never get bored, choose a different line, or let someone cut in front.

Line 1 explains one everyday thing (a queue) to a 10-year-old in a single
vivid sentence built on one analogy: a slow train made of people. Line 2
says in one sentence why that analogy works: what it maps (the order and
the waiting) and what it leaves out (boredom, choosing another line,
letting someone cut in).
